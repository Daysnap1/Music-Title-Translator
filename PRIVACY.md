# Privacy Policy: Music Title Translator

_Last updated: November 11, 2026_

Music Title Translator is a browser extension that translates song titles, artist names and album names on YouTube Music, Spotify and Apple Music.

## What data is used

- **Song titles, artist names and album names** shown on those three sites, and only those written in a script other than Latin and other than your chosen language's script, are sent to Google Translate (`translate.googleapis.com`) to be translated. Google's handling of that text is covered by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Your chosen target language** is stored in Chrome's sync storage so it follows your browser profile.
- **Translations** are cached in Chrome's local storage on your device so the same title isn't translated twice.

## What is not collected

The extension does not collect, store or transmit any personal information, browsing history, account details, listening history or analytics. It has no servers of its own and no tracking. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone other than the translation request described above.

## Removing your data

Uninstalling the extension deletes its cache and settings.

Questions or concerns: open an issue on this project's GitHub repository.
